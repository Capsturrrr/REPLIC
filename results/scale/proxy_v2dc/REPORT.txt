LIMITS
  * path families are our own parameter ranges, not measurements of real networks
  * dc_microburst cross traffic is a synthetic on/off process in the proxy; it does not exist under netem (the netem backend keeps delay/jitter/loss/rate/buffer only)
  * with n=30, p99 interpolates between the two largest samples; report it as a point estimate
  * the proxy impairs the forward path (delay both ways, loss/queue forward only); netem on lo impairs both directions -- do not mix the two in one comparison
  * a single 1 MB transfer per run: short-flow behaviour (slow start, handshake) dominates on dc_microburst
  * confidence intervals describe the sampling error over drawn paths and run noise together, not any one real path
